Privacy and Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

1. Data controller

TARAVIA d.o.o., Savska cesta 32, 10000 Zagreb, Croatia, OIB (tax no.): 86403373968, email: info@taravia.hr, phone: +385 98 187 1534.

2. What data we collect

When you fill in the “Request a quote” form, we collect the data you enter: your name or company name, phone number, email address, type of service and the shipment and route details. The same applies when you contact us by email or phone.

3. Purpose and legal basis

We use your data only to answer your enquiry, prepare a quote and arrange transport. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract and performing the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation – GDPR).

4. Who receives the data

We do not sell your data or share it for marketing. To run the website and email we use trusted service providers, bound by appropriate data processing terms:

  • a form delivery provider – forwards form messages to our email;
  • a website hosting provider;
  • a domain and email forwarding provider;
  • a business email provider.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, with appropriate safeguards required by the GDPR.

5. How long we keep data

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed to respond and for any resulting cooperation; if we sign a contract, for as long as required by law (e.g. accounting rules).

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, restrict processing, object and data portability. Send your request to info@taravia.hr. If you believe your rights have been violated, you can lodge a complaint with the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (azop.hr).

7. Cookies

This website does not use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. Your browser only stores your choice from the cookie notice, so we do not ask you on every visit. This is essential for the site to work and you can delete it in your browser settings.

When you visit the site, the hosting providers may briefly log technical data such as IP address and browser type for security and technical purposes.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page.
